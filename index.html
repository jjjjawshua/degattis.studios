      How the 50/50 works

      Partnership terms · draft

      These terms are a draft. They become binding only in a written agreement we both sign before production.

      1. You keep your idea. Sending a design doesn't transfer anything. If we don't pick it within 90 days, it stays entirely yours.
      2. We develop it at our cost. CAD, prototypes, tooling and photography are on us. You see each stage and approve the final piece.
      3. Profit is split 50/50. Net profit on every unit sold: the price, less materials, manufacturing, packaging, shipping, payment fees and tax. Paid quarterly with a statement.
      4. You're credited. Your name goes on the product page, the packaging insert and the journal post about the piece.
      5. Exclusive while it's on sale. While we sell the object, you agree not to license the same design elsewhere. If we stop making it for 12 months, the rights come back to you.

      About

      An independent studio that makes everyday objects feel collectible: openers, rings, clips and things for the desk, each with one vermilion part.

      Around the objects there's a world of images and sound: optical artwork, short films and KRAM Radio. The objects are hard and exact. Everything around them is soft.

      From sketch to steel

      How we work
      Sketch

      Start on paper

      Every object starts as a pencil outline around something real: a bottle cap, a key, a finger.

      CAD

      Model in millimetres

      The drawing becomes a CAD model, tested in 3D for weight, edges and how the vermilion part fits.

      Make

      Cut, machine, finish

      Waterjet, CNC and hand finishing in 316L stainless or 6061 aluminium. Then the screw goes in.
