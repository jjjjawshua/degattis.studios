Everything soft is colour. The only sharp things are vermilion and type.

The direction takes the quality you liked in the Echo Sonata cover: saturated colour pushed behind frosted, reeded glass until it reads as a memory of colour. degattis studios' objects are hard, machined metal. The graphics are the opposite, and that contrast is the identity. Vermilion #E9553A is never blurred. It sits on top of the fog the way a vermilion screw sits in a steel part.

04 — Type

One typeface, three weights

Everything is set in Inter Tight: the lowercase degattis studios logo and headlines at 700 with tight tracking, reading text at 400, and small technical labels at 500 in caps. One family keeps the type quiet so the colour can be loud.

06 — Rules

Keep it quiet around the colour

Do

  • Leave at least a third of every frame as open fog.
  • Keep the blobs in a clear arrangement: a ring, a grid, a column. The structure is what keeps the blur from looking accidental.
  • Set the logo white and large. Low contrast is intended. Small mono labels go in graphite.
  • Use exactly one sharp vermilion element per piece.
  • Put real technical information in mono: codes, millimetres, edition numbers.

Don't

  • Don't blur, tint or gradient the vermilion.
  • Don't put body text on the artwork; it belongs on paper.
  • Don't run more than six signal colours in one image.
  • Don't add drop shadows, glows or 3D type to the logo, and never set it in caps.
  • Don't put the frosted treatment on product photos. Photograph the metal sharp and place it next to the fog.
